## 中
前段时间我做了人生第一个可以被大家看到的小产品，是一个谷歌浏览器插件，叫Idle Fishing。

发布的开始几天，这里的用户数一直是1，也就是我自己。我已经不抱任何希望了，想着第一次发布，跑通流程就好。

没想到今天一看，这里的用户数居然变成了3。

然后我们看用户的安装数。

居然有17个人安装了我做的插件。

虽然在全世界范围内，只有这17个人安装了。但这对于一个第一次做自己的产品的人来说，是一个非常大的鼓励。


我现在来介绍一下这款插件。

这款插件是可以让你在浏览大多数网页的同时进行钓鱼的一个小游戏。

现在让我们点击插件图标，在弹出框里输入你的昵称和密码。

这个昵称和密码不需要注册，你可以随便设置。即使你是第一次使用。

比如，昵称是test2，密码是1234

然后，点击确认签名。在弹出框里这一串就是你的签名。你可以记一下。

然后我们点击开始钓鱼。

在屏幕任意位置点击，来模拟鱼钩落到水里的位置。

然后，我们会看到这里有了一个鱼漂。

接下来就可以不管它，正常浏览网页了。包括点击，刷新，进入新的网页等操作都是不影响的

等待一段时间，在正常情况下会是等待时间5到20分钟左右。

当我们看到鱼漂下沉小时后，会出现一个指示灯。

这个指示灯会在红、黄、绿三色之间随机变化。

我们长按指示灯周围会出现一个进度条。进度条会随时间而变长，颜色也会变化。松开的话，会慢慢变回去。

当进度条的颜色和指示灯的颜色相同时，会加分。不同时，会扣分。

当分数达到一定值时，钓鱼就成功了。

然后，我们点击我的鱼池，就会看到我们刚刚钓到的鱼了，你看它正在我们的鱼池里游呢。

如果，我们鱼池里的鱼太多了。我们可以点击“放入公共鱼池”，这样这个鱼就会在我们的鱼池里消失，出现在公共鱼池里。

在公共鱼池里大家都可以看到你的鱼，你也可以查找或删除你的鱼。

这是一个既无聊有稍微有那么一点意思的小游戏吧。

重要的是，它完全免费。没有任何需要支付的地方。甚至你想为我捐赠一杯咖啡支持一下都没有办法。

因为我还没有搞定支付的方法。

所以，如果在浏览网页时有些无聊，欢迎在谷歌插件商店搜索“Idle Fishing”，并将你钓到的鱼放入公共鱼池，让大家看见。

如果你喜欢这款插件，请在谷歌插件商店里评分或者点赞这条视频。有任何意见欢迎留下评论评论或者直接将意见发到我的邮箱。我都会看到的。

这对我非常重要。

谢谢你的观看。

## 英


**(开场白 / The Hook & Intro)** Hey everyone! A little while ago, I launched my very first public product out into the wild. It’s a Google Chrome extension called **Idle Fishing**.

For the first few days, my active user count was literally just 1—and that was me. Honestly, I didn't have high hopes; I just wanted to learn how the whole publishing process works.

But... I checked again today, and that number jumped to 3! Then I looked at the total installs... 17 people actually installed my extension! Now, I know 17 might not sound like a lot on a global scale, but for someone releasing their very first project, this is incredibly encouraging.

**(产品介绍与教程 / The Product & Tutorial)** So today, I want to show you guys what this extension is all about. Basically, **Idle Fishing** is a mini-game that lets you go fishing right on your screen while you browse the web.

Let’s click the extension icon. First, you just punch in a nickname and password. No sign-up or email required, you can literally make it up on the spot, even if it's your first time. For example, nickname: `test2`, password: `1234`.

Next, click **"Confirm"**, and you'll get this unique signature string right here in the pop-up. You might want to save that!

Now, we hit **"Start Fishing"**. Just click anywhere on your screen to simulate casting your line and dropping the hook into the water. And there it is—your bobber!

Now for the best part: you can just ignore it and browse the web like normal! Clicking, refreshing, or going to a new page won't interrupt your fishing at all.

Usually, you just wait around 5 to 20 minutes. Once the bobber sinks and disappears, an indicator light will pop up. This light will randomly switch between red, yellow, and green.

If you long-press the light, a progress bar appears. It gets longer and changes color the longer you hold it, and slowly shrinks back down when you let go. The goal is to match the color of the progress bar with the indicator light. If they match, you score points! If they don't, you lose points. _(你可以根据实际游戏逻辑调整这句)_

Once you hit the target score... Boom! You've successfully caught a fish.

**(展示成果与社交功能 / The Reward & Social Features)** If we click on **"My Fish Pond"**, you can actually see the fish we just caught swimming around in there.

Got too many fish? You can click **"Release to Public Pond"**. It’ll disappear from your personal pond and swim over to the global public pond. In the public pond, everyone can see the fish you've caught, and you can even search for or delete your own fish.

**(结尾与呼吁行动 / Outro & Call to Action)** It’s a simple, slightly silly idle game, but I think it’s pretty fun.

And the most important part? It is 100% free. There are no paywalls, no microtransactions—heck, you couldn't even buy me a coffee right now to support me because I haven't figured out how to set up payments yet! _(这里可以配合一个无奈笑的表情或者音效，YouTuber常用这种自嘲拉近距离)_

So, if you ever get a little bored while browsing the web, go search for **"Idle Fishing"** on the Chrome Web Store. Catch some fish and drop them in the public pond for everyone to see!

If you enjoy the extension, I would really appreciate a 5-star rating on the Chrome store, and a like on this video. If you have any feedback or suggestions, please drop a comment down below or shoot me an email. I read all of them, and it means the world to me.

Thanks so much for watching, and happy fishing!
